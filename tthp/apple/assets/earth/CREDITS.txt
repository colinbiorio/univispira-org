Imagery for the Earth and the way down to Wilmington (tthp/apple, stages/earth.js).
All of it was fetched from NASA GIBS (Global Imagery Browse Services, NASA ESDIS),
https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/ (WMS, EPSG:4326), on 18 November 2026, then checked,
re-encoded (metadata stripped) and resized. NASA imagery is public domain; please credit NASA.

day_4096.jpg, day_2048.jpg   NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation with shaded relief
                             and bathymetry (Reto Stockli; July 2004; GIBS layer BlueMarble_ShadedRelief_Bathymetry,
                             fetched at 8192 x 4096), the open sea blended toward the plain Blue Marble
                             (GIBS layer BlueMarble_NextGeneration) so it reads as it does from orbit.
night_4096.jpg, night_2048.jpg  NASA Earth Observatory, Black Marble 2016 (Suomi NPP VIIRS day/night band;
                             GIBS layer VIIRS_Black_Marble); only the lights of towns kept.
clouds_4096.jpg, clouds_2048.jpg  The clouds of one real day, 24 October 2025: VIIRS Corrected Reflectance
                             True Color from Suomi NPP and NOAA-20 (GIBS layers VIIRS_SNPP_CorrectedReflectance_TrueColor,
                             VIIRS_NOAA20_CorrectedReflectance_TrueColor), the darker of the two per pixel (sun glint
                             moves, clouds hardly) minus the cloud-free Blue Marble; the dark polar night filled with
                             a made-up cover.
water_2048.png               MODIS land/water mask, MOD44W (GIBS layer MODIS_Terra_L3_Land_Water_Mask, 2015).
r1.jpg, r1_water.png         The Carolinas (30.5-38 N, 84-74 W): the same Blue Marble layers at 500 m; MOD44W.
r2.jpg, r2_water.png         Cape Fear (33.55-34.85 N, 78.75-77.25 W), 16 October 2025: NASA HLS S30
                             (Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2, 30 m; GIBS layer HLS_S30_Nadir_BRDF_Adjusted_Reflectance),
                             holes filled from r1; MOD44W. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel-2 data (2025).
r3.jpg, r3_water.png         Wilmington (34.12-34.32 N, 78.00-77.76 W), the same HLS S30 day, smoothed;
                             water from its colours and MOD44W. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel-2 data (2025).
rc_clouds.jpg                The south-east's clouds of 24 October 2025 at 375 m (VIIRS SNPP, as above).

Everything closer than the satellites see (the neighbourhood, its houses and trees, the clouds one
flies through, the kitchen, the marble, the cutting board) is made in code (stages/earth/*.js);
no real house or address is shown.
