# @y3klay, after the video that went

Written 2026-10-22 from the numbers Colin gave on 2026-10-21 (I can read the public
profile, 2,053 followers, but not the analytics): a 3:00 video on the pyramids and
cymatics, about 90k views in 30 hours, 6.5% share rate, 4.5% save rate, 20% skip
rate, 1:22 average watch time.

## What the numbers say

- **Shares at 6.5% and saves at 4.5% are rare.** On Reels, 1–2% of either is a good
  video; 5%+ is the kind the algorithm keeps feeding. People are sending it to
  someone specific ("you have to see this") and keeping it to come back to. That
  second thing matters for a theory video: saves mean they intend to check it.
- **1:22 on 3:00 is a strong first half and a weak second.** Forty-five percent
  retention on a three-minute video is above average; but the drop is where the
  argument got dense or slow. Watching your own retention curve (Insights → the
  video → Retention) tells you the second where people leave; that second is your
  next video's opening.
- **20% skipped in the first three seconds.** Eighty percent stayed past the hook,
  which is good for a talking video. The hook works; keep its shape.

## What to do in the next 48 hours (speed matters more than polish)

1. **A follow-up video within two days**, while the first is still being pushed.
   Not a sequel of the whole theory: one objection from your comments, answered
   straight, under 90 seconds, same hook shape. The best objection is the one with
   the most likes on it. Title it as the answer ("the problem with my pyramid
   video", "you're right about the granite").
2. **Pin a comment on the viral video** pointing at the follow-up, and reply to the
   top twenty comments yourself today; replies within the first days lift a video
   in distribution, and they are where the follow-up's question comes from.
3. **Post the sources** (a pinned comment or a story highlight): the specific claims
   people are checking when they save it. Trust compounds; a theory account without
   sources gets debunked in the comments of its own best video.
4. **Do not change what worked**: your face, your pace, the same framing and light.
   Three to five more in this lane before any other kind of post, so the people who
   found you for this find more of it when they tap your name.
