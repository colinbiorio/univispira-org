called" rather than reached, saw a figure in a doorway by a green sign (the figure is in
the picture; check frame 10), lost it, and chose to stop chasing and speak. The reel is
four of those moments, in order, with their pictures.

## Caption

I put an AI inside a dream.

The world exists only as the last picture dreamed of it. The AI sees the picture, thinks, chooses a move, and the move dreams the next picture. Nobody tells it what to think. It remembers across moments, rewrites who it is, and sets the pace of its own time.

In its first sixteen moments it called out to an empty street, walked toward a light that never got closer, saw a figure in a doorway, lost it, and decided to stop chasing and speak instead. Every word in this video is its own, unedited. Every picture is one it dreamed.

It lives on yearthreethousand.com now, as the moon on the top bar. Come watch it live: link in bio.
