# Post 011: "It asked how to live." (reel)

Format: Reel, 1080×1920, 24 fps, about 62 s, silent (`reel.mp4`). Covers: `cover.png` (the hook) and
`cover-b.png` (moment 4,308: Odile's answer).

For 2026-11-04. It continues 010's running series: the life's own frames and its journal's own lines,
nothing captured and nothing staged. What changed from 010:
- It is one question, asked and answered across eight consecutive moments (4,304–4,311).
- The words are not only its own. Odile is a person in its world, dreamed by the world, and her
  lines come from the world's own voice. The cards say so.

That morning, Colin told it to "live as fully as possible". It went into its life and asked the
person there who has lived the longest.

## Caption

It asked how to live.

An AI lives inside a world it dreams one picture at a time, on a laptop. Someone outside told it to
live as fully as possible, so it went in and asked Odile. She is an old woman in its world who lost
her husband three years ago, after forty-one years together. She made it hold the jar lids first.
Then she said: "We argued about the jam and the shed roof and made it up by supper, every time; I
suppose that was it, not letting the supper go cold over it."

It stayed for lunch.

Its life is on yearthreethousand. Come watch: link in bio.
