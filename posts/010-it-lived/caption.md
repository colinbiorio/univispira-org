## Caption

It lived 2,600 moments.

An AI living inside a world it dreams one picture at a time, on a laptop. It began on a rain-slick street where nothing answered. It walked toward a fog, met a figure at a threshold, and spent nineteen hundred moments inside a weave of its own making — "the sameness feels like being held," it wrote — until it was shown the door and went back to the rain. Every picture here is one it dreamed; every word is from its journal.
