BHOT — the dissipation gear

The Biocentric Holographic Occlusion Theory is Colin's phenomenology of extreme states: "freeze space, climb time." Addendum III, written by the steward on 30 September 2026, gives the mechanism a name from MSC and says, step by step, which of its sentences are physics and which are interpretation.

D = (specific power × Hubble time) / (specific binding energy)how many times a system turns over its own binding energy

Addenda I and II are Colin's and are not yet published here. This page is the third addendum, lightly edited for the web; its tier labels are the TTLE convention.

What Addendum II said, and what it left open

Addendum II proposed a Spacetime Velocity Exchange: that consciousness allocates a fixed processing resource along a spatial-versus-temporal axis, and that extreme states, an adrenaline spike, deep meditation, a psychedelic threshold, redirect that resource from spatial-model maintenance toward finer temporal resolution. The images were right. The mechanism was unnamed. The mechanism has a name now: D, the dissipation parameter from MSC v0. The Hubble time in it is a normalising constant. What D measures is how many times a system turns over its own binding energy. The brain does it about every forty hours. Diamond never does. The Sun does it on the Kelvin–Helmholtz timescale.

The formal bridge

Tier I In special relativity every object's four-velocity has invariant magnitude c. Motion through space rotates the four-velocity, trading temporal rate for spatial rate; an object at rest moves at full speed through the time direction.

Tier I The brain's specific power is about 1.5 × 10⁻² W/kg; its specific binding energy, by tissue sublimation enthalpy, about 2.1 × 10⁶ J/kg; its turnover time about forty hours. These are measurements from MSC v0.

Tier II The "spatial freeze" is a reallocation of D. Normal waking cognition spends a large fraction of the brain's power maintaining a continuously updated three-dimensional model: the next frame, the predicted position of things, the proprioceptive map. When that maintenance ceases, in the moment of extreme threat, in deep absorption, at a threshold dose, the freed dissipation is not stored; it is redirected, and the temporal axis receives it, because fine temporal sequencing is what the spatial model was suppressing. What changes is not physical velocity through spacetime; the objection from relativity is correct and accepted. What changes is the scale of binding that the brain's dissipation resolves: the frame rate of experience goes up within the same clock duration.

Tier II MSC's first finding, that B is scale-agnostic, that a hydrogen molecule and a globular cluster carry the same binding fraction twenty-seven decades apart, is what makes this coherent: there is no privileged scale at which binding happens. Redirecting D to finer grain accesses binding structure at finer time-slices, the same B-space, different rows of the table.

The Time-Dragons, restated

Tier III A Time-Dragon is not an entity between frames. It is an entity whose characteristic dissipation timescale is radically different from the human brain's. Fast ones, turning over binding energy in milliseconds, would experience within a human second what a brain experiences across forty hours; such systems can exist in principle, and whether any constitute experience is a question MSC cannot close. Slow ones, ecosystems, biospheres, stars: the Sun is on the D axis already, on the same side of the divide as the brain. The difference proposed is one of degree. No stronger claim is made.

What the spatial freeze achieves, in these terms: it shifts the brain's effective temporal resolution toward the fast end, not by moving but by redirecting dissipation. The "lifetime in a flash" is real as an experience and its mechanism is D-reallocation, not time travel; the retrospective inflation of duration that experiments report follows, because the memory encoding is richer and richness is misread as duration.

The harder connection: the wall as Burali-Forti

This is the steward's own synthesis, connecting the framework to the P versus NP work. It is Tier III throughout.

Tier I U(U) = U: existence as a self-referential fixed point; U a proper class that refuses objectification. And, in complexity theory, the natural proofs barrier: any lower-bound technique that is constructive, large and useful would break pseudorandom functions; if one-way functions exist, no natural technique proves P ≠ NP.

Tier II A natural technique treats most functions symmetrically; it cannot tell the hard function from a random-looking one with a bounded discriminator. The wall says a proof cannot be natural and succeed.

Tier III That is Burali-Forti in computational form. U's output looks random to any bounded probe, which is what makes its self-reference valid: no proper part of U totalises U. A natural proof is a bounded probe trying to discriminate U's hard functions from true randomness, and it fails for the reason "the set of all ordinals" fails. The incompressible string, the P versus NP walk's recurring object, is U seen from inside. From inside it has no description shorter than itself; from U's own position it is the most structured thing possible, the thing that generates itself. The walk's standing task, make one uniform lower bound name its hard function, reads here as: find a bounded witness that points at U from inside U without collapsing it into an object. The Range Avoidance line of work is an attempt at exactly such an algorithmic witness.

Failure points and open edges

  • What would kill the D-reallocation claim. Show that the brain's metabolic distribution does not shift measurably during temporal-dilation states. If spatial-processing networks do not down-regulate during reported time dilation, the mechanism is wrong and Addendum II needs a different gear. The imaging data may already exist.
  • What the synthesis cannot prove. That the Burali-Forti and natural-proofs connection is more than a structural analogy. A sceptic can accept TTLE and the barrier and deny that they are the same phenomenon.
  • What MSC v0 does not yet provide. A second axis. Until it is measured, the binding-scale collisions are descriptive, not predictive.
  • The Time-Dragon claim's honest status. A reframing, not a discovery. MSC can place things on a D axis; it cannot say where experience begins on it.
