The claim, in one sentence

Existence is a self-creating fixed point whose internal mechanism is binding, and the human word love is the first-person name for that mechanism. That sentence is from the paper, and the paper is explicit that it is an Tier III interpretation resting on Tier I theorems and measurements through Tier II structural observations.

The rule this site never breaks

Tier I — theorem and measurementStandard results: Burali-Forti, Lévy–Montague reflection, Lawvere's fixed-point theorem, anti-foundation; the invariant magnitude of four-velocities; confinement's share of hadron mass; measured values.
Tier II — structural observationClaims that distinct established results share a form. Checkable by inspection, falsifiable by counterexample, and readings rather than theorems.
Tier III — interpretationProposals: the strong reading of the Somethingness Argument, binding as what value-language tracks, the cosmological closure. Carried by argument, not proof.

Every page here labels its claims with these three marks. A reader is never asked to accept a theorem on faith or an interpretation as a theorem.
